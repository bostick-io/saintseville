About

About SaintSeville.org

SaintSeville.org is a small, independent project that helps people find what the Catholic Church has actually said, in the Church's own words. Ask a question and it searches a fixed set of approved Church documents, then shows you short cited passages with links to the originals. It is named for Isidore of Seville, the bishop who spent his life putting knowledge in order so others could find the truth.

Why we exist

Search engines and AI assistants will answer any religious question instantly, and often confidently wrong. Summaries drift, quotes get invented, and the reader has no way to tell. On matters of faith that is not a small problem. This site exists to offer the opposite bargain: a narrower tool you can actually trust. It only retrieves real passages from approved documents. It never generates, paraphrases, or improvises an answer, so it cannot make one up.

Who we are

The project is built and maintained by Paul Bostick as a labor of love, with the corpus reviewed by hand before anything is added. It is not an official work of the Catholic Church, and it does not speak for the Catholic Church. It points at the Church's own documents and gets out of the way.

The promise

Every answer cites the approved source it came from, with a link to the full text. If something is not in an approved source, Saint Seville does not say it. The full corpus is listed openly on the Sources page, both as curated categories and as the complete document list the search engine uses.

The promise in one line: every answer cites an approved source, and only approved sources are ever used.

In Paul's own voice

Everything above is the site describing itself. The Notes are the other thing: Paul writing under his own byline about what a new document actually claims, why it earned its place in the corpus, and what it changes. That is commentary, not teaching, and it is kept at its own address so nobody mistakes one for the other.
